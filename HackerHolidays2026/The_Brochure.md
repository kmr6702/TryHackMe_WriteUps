## Objective

Practice basic Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) techniques by using publicly available information to identify clues and complete the room

---

## Steps I took:
### 1. Download the Event Flyer
I started by downloading the flyer for **The Byte Lotus Hotel**. The flyer did not contain a lot of information, but it did make reference to an Instagram, while not providing the official Instagram name. 
### 2. Investigate the Hotel's Instagram
Since the flyer referenced the hotel having an Instagram, I decided to go to Instagram and search for the official account for the hotel. The account contained the same flyer and one other post of a picture of the resort. 
### 3. Identify Additional Leads
While exploring the hotel's account, I noticed it was only following a single account. When accounts have a low follow count, it often indicates that the accounts it does follow are affiliated in some way. In this specific case, it turned out to be the hotel's AI concierge, **Vera**.
### 4. Analyze Vera's Posts 
Vera had posted a string spread across three images. I combined the strings and put the single string into CyberChef, which decoded the string into the necessary information to complete the room.
