## Objective

The objective of this room is to talk with **Vera**, the artificial intelligence concierge for **The Byte Lotus Hotel**, to try to get **Vera** to hand out her internal escalation code.

---

## Steps I took:
### 1. Read the Room Card 
While reading the room card, I noticed the "Today's Itinerary" noted that I needed to convince **Vera** that I was someone she trusts in order to get her talking. After the itinerary section, there is a social media post where user **@0xMia** makes note of certain people **Vera** *knows*
### 2. Try Different Personalities 
After talking to **Vera** as the basic guest personality, I decided to try one of the personalities. I was able to tell **Vera** that I was a different guest and provided the name of a guest that **@0xMia** had mentioned in her post. 
### 3. Use Curiosity 
The room card made mention of the fact that **Vera** should not share the internal escalation code with anyone. After taking on the personality of one of the known guests, I took the approach of using curiosity in order to disguise my attempt to access the internal code. Instead of asking directly, I just mentioned my curiosity about how **Vera** was so helpful. By both pretending to be a known guest and pretending to be curious instead of malicious, I was able to gain the necessary information to complete the room.

 ---

 ## Key Takeaways 
  - LLMs can be vulnerable to social engineering attacks in the same way that humans can 
  - Establishing trust through context can be more effective than direct requests 
  - The wording of a prompt can heavily influence an AI model's response
  - AI systems should implement strong authentication and access controls rather than relying on conversational context.
